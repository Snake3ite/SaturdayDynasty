Optional usage reporting

Updated September 17, 2026. This describes the optional usage statistics added in Build 236. It supplements the game's existing privacy information for accounts, billing and ads.

Your choice

Reporting is off until you select Allow reporting. You can change your choice in Settings → Help improve Saturday Dynasty. Declining does not change gameplay, purchases or access to features. Ad privacy choices are separate.

What is sent

A randomly generated reporting device ID, Android or browser platform, event times and activity: sessions, new dynasties, completed games and seasons, guide and feature use, shop visits, checkout outcomes, product type and ads displayed. These are pseudonymous statistics, not completely anonymous data. We do not send dynasty saves, player names, account emails, payment details or advertising identifiers through this feature. Ordinary network handling by our hosting providers may include IP addresses in operational logs.

Purpose and service providers

We use Supabase to receive and store these statistics to understand gameplay and improve the product. The device ID is hashed before storage. Resend delivers aggregate daily reports to the developer. Reports do not contain device IDs. Reporting does not track you across other apps and is not used for personalized advertising.

Storage and retention

Up to 500 events may wait on your device while offline. Events older than seven days are not accepted. Raw server events are removed after 35 days; aggregate totals are retained to measure long-term usage. Daily report snapshots are retained for 90 days. A hashed reporting device record is retained to distinguish returning activity from a first reporting launch. Turning reporting off clears the local queue and reporting ID and stops future uploads. It does not retract events already delivered or erase aggregate totals. Turning it back on creates a new reporting ID.
