# Competitor findings for Saturday Dynasty Football

Research date: October 15, 2026. Scope: official Google Play/App Store listings, developer websites and the player reviews visible on those listings. These apps were not installed or played during this review. No verified revenue, profit, acquisition-spend, retention or purchase-conversion data was available. Public downloads are cumulative milestones, not monthly active players.

## What is supported by public evidence

| App | Audience signal | Product proposition | Monetization evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retro Bowl College | 1M+ Android downloads | College coaching attached to an established Retro Bowl brand; 250 teams. | US iOS listing: $0.99 Unlimited Version and coaching-credit packs from $1.99 to $9.99. These are not verified Android prices or proof of sales volume. |
| The Program: College Football | 500K+ Android downloads | Scouting/recruiting, staff, practices, weekly plans and progression toward championships. | Rewarded ads disclosed on Google Play. US iOS listing includes $3.99 Season Pass products, $5.99 recruiting points and staff-token purchases. Product names do not establish a subscription or purchase frequency. |
| College Football Coach: Career | 100K+ Android downloads | Career simulation, customizable universes/rosters/coaches, editors and long-term statistical history. | Its developer describes it as completely free and open source. Useful demand evidence; not a verified commercial revenue benchmark. |

Sources: [Retro Bowl College on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.newstargames.retrobowlcollege), [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/retro-bowl-college/id1632904520), [official developer page](https://www.newstargames.com/retro-bowl-college); [The Program on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.atomic.collegefootball), [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-program-college-football/id1586374382); [College Football Coach on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=antdroid.cfbcoach).

## Likely reasons for appeal — interpretation, not measured causation

- **Retro Bowl College:** recognizable branding and a clear football identity make the pitch easy to understand. The low-priced unlock offers a small first purchase, with optional credit purchases alongside it. Its existing franchise audience is an advantage a new solo-developed game cannot reproduce simply by copying its interface.
- **The Program:** the advertised decisions form a repeated coaching routine: recruit, develop, prepare, compete, improve the school. Resources and pass products align with that routine. This is a plausible repeat-engagement/revenue mechanism, not evidence of its actual margins.
- **College Football Coach:** customization and historical depth give committed simulation fans ownership over a career. Its free model and long public history also mean its cumulative downloads should not be treated as evidence that stronger monetization would preserve the same audience.

## Reviews identify opportunities, not population statistics

Visible Retro Bowl College reviews praise short sessions, customization and the existing franchise, while also reporting control/speed problems. The Program's visible reviews include recruiting scarcity and perceived unfair outcomes, alongside praise for improvements. College Football Coach reviews mention save limitations/crashes and interest in more strategic challenge. Selected reviews are neither a random sample nor proof of the underlying complaints.

The practical lesson is to compete on a trustworthy experience: understandable decisions, visible progress, reliable saves and rewards that actually arrive. Do not copy scarcity just because a competitor sells resources.

## Recommended order for Saturday Dynasty — proposals only

| Priority | Proposed improvement or test | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Finish and release verified startup/save/reward fixes before increasing acquisition. | First-game completion in linked opted-in sessions; save/reward failures. |
| 2 | Show a small number of useful coaching decisions and their outcomes: recruit movement, preparation, next opponent, season progress. Keep the full depth accessible. | Time/actions to first game; first-season continuation. |
| 3 | Test marketing hooks separately: rebuild a weak school, win a recruiting battle, or create a custom league. Use actual fictional-team gameplay. | Store conversion and retained players by source/creative, not installs alone. |
| 4 | Explain existing All Access/editor/supporter benefits clearly at relevant moments. Check the first purchase feels understandable and worthwhile before changing prices or adding currencies. | Verified purchase conversion, refunds and player complaints. |
| 5 | Offer optional rewarded ads at existing decision moments with exact benefits, clear caps and reliable grants. Preserve a satisfying no-ad path. | Offer-to-completion, reward grants, revenue per active reporting player and retention. |
| 6 | Encourage voluntary dynasty summaries/screenshots and community league sharing. | Community clicks and attributable returning players; clicks are not confirmed joins. |

Do not add a season pass, lower base resources, change live prices or promise higher revenue on the strength of this review alone. First measure the current release. An iPhone app could expand addressable reach, but both competitors being on iOS does not prove an iOS launch would be profitable for this project; browser/device compatibility remains the immediate route to verify.

Revenue relationship for planning: active players × ad revenue per active player + verified purchases. More cumulative downloads, a higher eCPM, or more features alone does not guarantee more profit. Acquisition cost, retention and support burden still matter.
