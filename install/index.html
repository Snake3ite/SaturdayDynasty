iPhone & iPad

Use Safari. Home-screen apps can't be added from other browsers on older iOS versions.

  1. Open saturdaydynasty.ctoolis.workers.dev in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen, or at the top on iPad.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  4. Open Saturday Dynasty from your home screen. It runs full screen, like any other app.

Your dynasty is saved on this iPhone. Sign in from the game's menu if you want to sync it with another device.

Android

The best experience on Android is the free Google Play app, with offline play and native sharing.

  1. Get Saturday Dynasty Football on Google Play.
  2. Or, to play in the browser: open the site in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

Windows & Mac

Install it as a desktop app from Chrome or Edge, or just keep a bookmark.

  1. Open saturdaydynasty.ctoolis.workers.dev in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow), or open the browser menu and choose Install Saturday Dynasty, under Cast, save and share in Chrome or Apps in Edge.
  3. It opens in its own window and gets a Start menu or Dock icon.

On a Mac with Safari: choose File → Add to Dock. Or press Ctrl/⌘+D to bookmark it.
