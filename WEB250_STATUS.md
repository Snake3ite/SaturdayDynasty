# Web Build 250

Build 250 synchronizes custom conferences and custom schedules across the Android and browser editions.

- Gives each conference a permanent save-safe ID while allowing Commissioner Mode to rename its display name and abbreviation.
- Migrates existing saves without changing legacy conference membership data.
- Adds a 14-week custom schedule builder with 12 games and two bye weeks.
- Locks completed games, conference games and protected rivalries.
- Prevents duplicate opponents and prevents a team from being booked twice in a week when generated team schedules are available.
- Shows unavailable teams as disabled choices and immediately reserves selections across the rest of the schedule.
- Improves bye-week presentation and mobile schedule editing.

Validation: 90 release checks pass and 172 native assets match. The Android debug APK compiles successfully.
